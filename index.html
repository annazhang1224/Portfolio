Deal work 2025
Analyst, team of four

The target's growth depended on two customers, so the price had to reflect that

A private equity buyer wanted to know whether a UK software business could sustain 20% annual growth. I built the market model, ran twelve customer interviews and rebuilt the revenue forecast by customer cohort, which showed concentration risk the management forecast hid.

Result: the client revised its offer and negotiated an earn-out.

Deal work 2024
Analyst

Two thirds of the synergies were in procurement, not headcount

Two mid-market distributors were combining. I mapped overlapping suppliers across both, sized each saving and sequenced them into a first-100-days plan.

Result: the board approved the plan and procurement was the first workstream to start.

Pro bono 2023
Project lead, team of five

The charity should drop two programmes to fund the one that works

An education charity had flat income and five programmes. I led a student team through cost-per-outcome analysis and funder interviews, and took three options to the trustees.

Result: trustees adopted the recommendation and moved the budget the following year.

LSE Strategic Consulting Society 2022 to 2023
President

Student projects got better once someone senior read the deck before the client did

As president I ran a committee of twelve, pairing student teams with real clients. I introduced a midpoint review with alumni working in consulting, and went out to find clients rather than waiting for requests.

Result: client projects went from 6 to 11 a year, each with an alumni reviewer.
